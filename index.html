About Me

I Build Software People Rely On

I'm a software engineer with a BSc in Software Engineering from Mbarara University of Science and Technology. As a founding partner at Algosys, I design and maintain Algosys CBS, a core banking platform for banks, SACCOs and money-lending businesses, and I onboard and deploy instances for new clients.

My work goes beyond code. At Streamline Health Tech I worked as an Associate Product Manager and Designer, turning client requirements into specifications, running user acceptance testing and managing releases. I also served as a District IT Support Officer for the Electoral Commission during Uganda's General Elections.
